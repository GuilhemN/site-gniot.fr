About Me

I am a researcher at PQShield. I recently completed my PhD there under the supervision of Thomas Prest (PQShield) and Pierre-Alain Fouque (Université de Rennes). Before that, I graduated from ENS Lyon, France, and EPFL, Switzerland, with a double Master’s degree in Computer Science.

My work focuses on cybersecurity and cryptography, with a particular interest in privacy, applied cryptography, and software security. I enjoy working across a wide range of topics, from analysing the security of protocols and building efficient, secure software to designing theoretical yet practical cryptographic schemes.

Check my resume to learn more about my background.

Research Interests

  • Cryptography: protocol design, cryptography engineering
  • Cybersecurity: pen-testing, threat modelling
